Privacy Policy for Sthitaprajna

Effective Date: October 31, 2026
Key Summary: Sthitaprajna is built with a strict 100% Offline-First and Privacy-Preserving architecture. All your personal reflections, voice notes, journal entries, and somatic check-ins remain exclusively on your local device. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your personal data on any remote server.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Sthitaprajna ("we", "us", or "our"), published by Naveen Kumar, handles your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Zero Personal Data Collection:

  • We do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or cloud sign-in.
  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or IP addresses.
  • We do not use third-party analytics trackers, tracking cookies, or advertising SDKs.

3. Local Device Permissions

Sthitaprajna requests certain device permissions solely to provide on-device features you explicitly initiate:

  • Audio Recording (Microphone): Used exclusively for on-device voice journaling and dictation. Audio recordings are stored in your device's private app storage and are never uploaded to any external server.
  • Camera: Used exclusively for on-device video reflection drills. Video preview data stays in device volatile memory and is never transmitted or shared.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to deliver scheduled daily reminders and pause intervals configured by you on your local device.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Used to provide tactile somatic feedback during breathing and calm drills.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All user entries (journal logs, character ledger, bookmarks, reflection notes) are stored strictly locally in encrypted/private device databases (Hive & SQLite). You have complete control over your data. If you clear app data or uninstall the application, all local records are permanently removed from your device.

5. Third-Party Services and Ads

  • No Advertisements: Sthitaprajna is 100% ad-free.
  • External Links: When you choose to view wisdom resources via external links (e.g., YouTube or educational websites), interactions on those external sites are subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Sthitaprajna does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe and suitable for general audiences seeking mindfulness, philosophical reflection, and self-mastery.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date on this page.
